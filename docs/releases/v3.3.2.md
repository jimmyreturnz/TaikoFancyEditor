# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.3.2

The slow-playback "kick lands late" report found and fixed, and wheel scrolling during playback made smooth.

## Fixed

- **Hitsounds now default to +22ms against the music**, which is what "the kick lands late at 25%" actually was. It was never the time-stretch: charts put each note about 22ms before the attack as this app decodes the song, a gap nobody hears at 1.0x and roughly 90ms of real time at 25%, hitsound first. Measured with the note-to-attack harness over 60 random installed taiko maps: median **+22ms on MP3** and **+23ms on OGG**, so it is one flat offset for every map and not a codec's gapless delay. A saved Hitsound offset is kept; Reset defaults picks up the new value.
- **Scrolling with the wheel during playback no longer freezes the playhead.** While a fast spin's seeks were held back to merge, the engine kept reporting the old position, and the playhead's never-move-backwards rule pinned it in place: 110 of 359 frames frozen with a notch every 25ms, now 0. The audio of the last seek in a spin also now starts where the playhead already is rather than 50ms behind it, which took the freeze after a fast spin from about 60ms to none. Frame timing was never the cause — 0.2ms of work per frame throughout.
- **Right-clicking a fake slider's red line removes only that line.** The slider stays; right-clicking the slider itself still removes both. Works for the line on a plain fake slider and for a Don/Kat's 60000 BPM squash line, which is the one that used to take the slider with it.
- **The offset calibrator takes key taps as soon as it opens.** Every button in it refuses focus so a tap can't press one — which also meant nothing had focus on open, and taps went nowhere until you clicked the dialog.

## New

- **Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] nudge the hitsound offset by 1ms while playing**, saved as you go. Tapping can't calibrate this one — hitsounds and music leave through the same buffer — so it is tuned by ear: slow the chart to 25%, where every millisecond of error lasts four, and nudge until the two hits fuse. Both keys can be rebound in Settings.
