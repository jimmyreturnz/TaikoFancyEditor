# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.4.1

A minor audio and UI fix release.

## Fixed

- **The volume sliders in Settings change the volume as you drag them.** They used to reach the editor only after the dialog closed, and song select's preview only when a song was selected again. They now reach the song, the hitsounds and the preview while you drag. Nothing is saved until OK, and Cancel puts the old volume back.
- **The rate mod reaches song select's preview**, as it does in osu!: DT and HT keep the pitch, NC and DC resample it. The speed buttons do not affect the preview, because they are an editing aid and not a mod.
- **Swap Don ↔ Kat sits beside the Don / Kat pills** instead of on the mode row, which it pushed wider than the Transform dock so the buttons overlapped. It is also the same height as the pills now.

47 test modules, 1596 tests.
