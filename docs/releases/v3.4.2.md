# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.4.2

The in-app updater, the map's background behind the editor, a music player on song select, and a round of fixes from user reports.

## Updating to this version

**This is the last version you download by hand.** v3.4.1's updater still unpacks a new version into a separate folder next to the old one, so to get v3.4.2, click **Download Update**, close the app, and start `TaikoFancyArranger.exe` from the new `TaikoFancyArranger-3.4.2` folder. You can delete the old folder afterwards; your settings are not stored in it.

From v3.4.2 on, an update is one click: **Update and Restart** downloads the new version, checks it against the release's SHA-256, asks you to save unsaved difficulties, installs it into the folder you already run from, and reopens. If anything goes wrong mid-install, the old version is put back and reopened with a message. Keep the folder somewhere you can write to (not `Program Files`).

## Added

- **The map's background shows behind the Editor and Gimmick pages**, faded into the navy. Only the space around the views changes; the lanes stay solid, so ten open difficulties cost nothing extra. **Settings → Editor background → Background opacity** sets how strongly it shows (default 25%) and updates while you drag it.
- **Right-click the empty space below the views to Add view**, on both the Editor and Gimmick pages.
- **A music player on song select**: previous, play / pause, stop and next, beside the Group and sort pills. Previous and next walk a shuffled song order that is fixed for the session, and select that song in the list. **Space** pauses and resumes, except inside a search, where it types a space.
- **Reload this beatmap (F5)** in the Songs folder menu re-reads only the selected song's files.
- **Settings → Fancy Arranger → Animate transforms** turns off the glide when notes move to a new shape.
- **The note opacity preview in Settings uses the skin you picked**, updates as soon as you change it, and now includes a spinner. It reuses the editor timeline's own drawing, so it shows what the timeline shows.
- **AR and CS are sliders again** in the Fancy Arranger, beside their number boxes.

## Changed

- **Song select's preview always plays at 1.00x and the song's own pitch**, whatever mod is selected. v3.4.1 applied the rate mod to it; that was a mistake. Mods apply in the editor.
- **The glide in the Fancy Arranger is about 9x cheaper.** Each note look is drawn once and stamped: on Nbt-Hwt [The Pharaoh's Curse] (3000 notes) at 1920x1080, a frame went from 163ms to 18ms (p95 184ms → 20ms).
- **Vertical SV drag moves one 0.01 step per pixel** from where you pressed, instead of following the log axis under the cursor, which skipped most steps and could leave an 8-decimal value. A click that does not move leaves a typed value as it was.

## Fixed

- **Real drumrolls lost their track with a skin**: only the head and the end cap were drawn. A skin's `taiko-roll-middle` is a 1px column (2px at @2x), and scaled to a note's height it came out 0px wide. It now keeps at least one pixel, in the timeline, the gameplay preview and fake sliders alike.
- **Dragging a slider or the Transform dock's scrollbar in the Fancy Arranger cleared the selection.** Only a click on empty canvas, Esc, or an empty click on the timeline deselects now.
- **The CS box in the Fancy Arranger ignored the map's own CircleSize** and sat at 7, so Save and Export wrote CS 7 over every map. It now starts at the map's CS, and the grab area is the circle as drawn instead of a fixed size.
- **The triangle and star sat high of their Center**: the triangle by 40 osu!pixels, the star by 16. Both are centred on Center now.
- **Hitsounds were dropped at DT**: about a third of the notes at 1.5x were never voiced, because they fell between two time-stretch grains.
- **The mouse wheel stepped over an off-grid red line**, skipping the downbeat it was placed to mark. It now stops on it in both directions.
- **The snap grid after an off-grid red line followed the old timing**, and shifted as the view scrolled. It restarts on each red line now, and still reaches back before a map's first red line.
- **A menu's shortcut text was drawn over its label** ("Reload this beatmap F5").
