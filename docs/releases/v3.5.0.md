# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.0

A song select rebuilt around the chart, live themes, and the editor's views made to move.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **The chart scrolls under the art in song select.** The selected difficulty plays in a bare black lane under the background -- the real gameplay preview, so it scrolls with the map's own SV and BPM, at 67% of its usual height (50-75% in Settings). It appears with the song, like the difficulties, and its hit target sits nearer the left, since there is no drum to leave room for.
- **The beat stripe under the art is a seek bar.** Click, drag or scroll on it to move the preview anywhere in the song. It says nothing about it on screen.
- **Drag a view by its header to reorder it**, on the Editor and Gimmick pages. It lifts, the others glide out of the way and it eases into place; the arrows stay. Measured on *LuzeriA - Nbt-Hwt [The Pharaoh's Curse]*'s seven gimmick layers at 1920x1080: 1.1ms a frame, a frame every 8ms.
- **Themes change as they are picked**, with no restart, and Cancel puts the old one back. A switch takes about a quarter of a second on a full window. The pink theme is now **osu!**, grounded on #1f1e33, and **Lantern Rite**, **Gold** and **Monokai** are new.
- **A selected view is lit, and the others fade.** The focused view carries a slow running light round its rim; the other views on its page dim. **Esc**, or a click on the empty space under the views, lets go of it -- and the next Esc goes back to the song list as before. The same light runs round the selected song and difficulty, Left/Right flies it between the two lists, and the page tabs catch a sheen on hover.
- **Esc in a text box only leaves the box**, in the window or a dialog; the next Esc does what it always did.
- **Views come back.** Going to the song list and returning to a difficulty restores the views that were open for it, in their order, on both pages, for the session.
- **View opacity**, in Settings: lower shows more of the map's background through every view.

## Changed

- **Song select is two columns.** The search, the Continue cards and the song list share one width; the art sits right under the pills at 16:9. Continue shows four cards of one size, each its chart's art at 16:9, and remembers four. The difficulty list keeps room for five difficulties until the window is too short for it.
- **Density is read against the chart's most common BPM**, the tempo in force for the most time (aleph-0 is mostly 250 for all its 400 and 140), one bar per 1% of the song, full at 16 notes in 4 beats. It used to be scaled to its own busiest bar, so one burst flattened the rest of the song.
- **A finisher plays its small note's sound under the big one** -- hitnormal with hitfinish, hitclap with hitwhistle -- as in game.
- **Finishers are a third bigger** in the Editor and Gimmick chart views, so they stand out in a dense row. The gameplay preview keeps osu!'s size.
- **Mod pills are grouped [HR EZ] [HD FL] [DT NC HT DC]**, each in its own colour. A lit EZ, HT or DC fills a darker green (white text at 4.6:1, against osu!'s 1.2:1). HT and DC light 75% too. Mod strings keep osu!'s order.
- **The kiai flash is softer**: a don or kat peaks at 0.10 white instead of 0.19. Shiny stacks are unchanged.
- **The gameplay preview's lane is the skin's `taiko-bar-right`**, its kiai glow `taiko-bar-right-glow` at half strength.
- **The mouse wheel scrolls the song anywhere on the Editor page**, as on the Gimmick page; the strip on the right still scrolls the page.
- **The Fancy Arranger's chart lane is drawn like the Editor's**, notes on a centred row.
- **Song rows show at most 20 difficulty dots**, then "+n".
- **"Hitsounds from" is gone** from the status bar; hitsounds come from the difficulty being edited.

## Fixed

- **Space in song select played the editor's song** after a trip into a chart. It plays the song list's song now.
- **The EGTS 2022 pack showed one difficulty and played nothing.** One chart's red lines have a beat length of 1.1e-319, which is an infinite BPM, and printing it crashed the list. It reads ∞ now; all ten charts load.
- **The song select preview stuttered.** It drew 62 frames a second and its clock jumped back 40ms five times a second, chasing a player that reports its position every 52ms. It draws 125 now, with no jumps.
- **Focusing a view shifted its snap ticks.** The rim took 2px from each side of the lane; it is drawn over the view now.
- **A pasted or dragged spinner changed length.** Its end time moved with nothing; it moves with the spinner now.
- **Picking another object to place did not show until the mouse moved.**
- **A right click while dragging a selection opened Add view**, and right clicks on the timing bar could open it too. Only the empty space under the views opens it now, and never mid-drag.
- **The view backgrounds on the Editor page were solid navy** whatever View opacity said.
