# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.2.0

The editor now owns its audio transport, and every view that draws a note can draw yours.

## New

### Audio playback rebuilt

Song playback no longer goes through Qt's media player. The app decodes the track itself and drives the sound card directly, which fixes the three things that made slow practice unreliable:

- **Changing speed no longer moves the playhead.** The old backend stalled on a rate change and lost real song time — measured at 114 ms on a 0.25x to 1.0x switch, which is what "the offset moves when I click a speed button" was.
- **Slowing down keeps the pitch.** 25/50/75% are time-stretched, not slowed like a record.
- **The playhead follows the sample actually leaving the device**, not a progress signal reported in whole milliseconds.

### Music offset calibration

**Settings → Audio → Calibrate…** plays a click track and asks you to tap along. It writes one number, the app's own output offset, and never touches any beatmap's offset. Tap with any key.

### Skins

**Settings → Skin** lists the taiko skins in your osu! Skins folder. A chosen skin supplies note art, drumroll pieces and hitsounds to the gameplay preview *and* the editor timeline layers, per element — a skin with notes but no drumroll art keeps its notes and gets the built-in drawing for the rest.

### The gameplay preview is the playfield

It draws the skin's bar, scrolling background, barlines and hit target, at osu!'s own proportions. Hittable notes disappear when they reach the hit position; drumrolls travel through; fake sliders leave their tail cap behind. Kiai pulses on the beat, on the colourable part of a note only, so the black outline and face stay as the skinner drew them.

### Note opacity

**Settings → Skin → Note opacity** sets how solid notes are drawn in the editor timeline layers, so you can trade the snap grid's readability against the notes'.

## Changed

- **Kiai and Sound Volume layer: no horizontal dragging.** Its lines belong to the milliseconds that put them there, so a drag only changes volume, and a double click types one. That volume now drives how loud the don and kat actually play — 100% full, 0% silent.
- **Barline layer: Don and Kat mirror separately.** One checkbox each, beside their own spacings.
- **Views can be reordered** with ▲ / ▼ in each view's chrome, on the Editor and gimmick pages.
- Note colours are pinned: don `#e54c2e`, kat `#438dab`, drumroll `#fbb706`.

## Fixed

- **Scrolling fast no longer produces a burst of hitsounds.** A window larger than 200 ms is treated as the seek it is and sounds nothing.
- **Seeking while paused no longer drifts backwards.** The music offset was being subtracted from every seek, not just from playback — invisible at 125 ms a notch, larger than the step on a high-BPM section.
- **The Settings dialog keeps its own scrolling.** It used to seek the editor behind it.
- **The gimmick page's scrollbar scrolls the page** instead of seeking.
- **Big drumrolls use `taikobigcircle`**, not the small circle stretched.
- **Skins apply everywhere at once.** Gimmick layers used to stay unskinned until Settings was applied again.
- **Kiai and volume layers share the page's zoom.** *Development note: SV and kiai layers reach 60 s on that page rather than 120 s, which is the narrowest range every layer agrees on.*
- Negative-length fake sliders draw their backwards extent to scale, and the gameplay preview reads the map's own `SliderMultiplier` and osu!taiko's note sizes.
