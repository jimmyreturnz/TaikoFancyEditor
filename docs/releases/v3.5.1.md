# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.1

Conversions that keep the chart's own speed, a smoother song select, and a playhead that no longer freezes when you scroll.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Keep SV.** Every gimmick structure is red lines, and a red line resets SV to 1.0x. Convert Notes > Barline notes has a **Keep SV** switch that writes the chart's own speed back after each one, following its curves: Generate SV's linear, Sin, Sin In/Out, Exp and True Exp sweeps are recognised and listed under **Detected SV curves**, where a wrong guess can be changed. Measured over ten installed maps, every note's SV comes out identical to the original.
- **Experimental Conversion**, a menu on the barline layer: **Convert Whole Map, Keep SV…** converts the whole difficulty to barline notes or an anti-barline wall in one undo, Keep SV always on. Under a wall, lines that change kiai or volume stay where they were. *Darling Game Over Love* Ura Oni: barline notes in 7.7s, anti-barline in 3.9s.
- **A difficulty row says which BPM it mostly is**: aleph-0 reads "125–400 (250)", as lazer counts it.
- **The layer bands on the Gimmick page share one View size**, its own setting, apart from the Editor's views.
- **Opening a chart from song select** brings a gameplay preview under the Editor pair.

## Changed

- **Song select moves.** Search results rise in row by row, the selection glides between rows, a new song's note counts roll up as its difficulties arrive, and the chart preview fades out of one song and into the next. The playhead rewinds and glides out to a new song's preview point. Buttons ripple where they are pressed, the page tabs' checked pill slides, and pages slide as they change.
- **The seek band under the art is the whole song**, not the chart up to its last note, and its strip is tall enough to grab.
- **Play morphs into pause and back.** Stop is gone from song select's player.
- **Continue fills four cards** from a longer memory, so a deleted chart no longer leaves the row a card short.
- **Toasts leave faster** (160ms, was 400) and rise in.
- **The kiai lift on the art is softer**, and the seek band no longer draws a trail or rings over the kiai marks.

## Fixed

- **Scrolling while playing froze the playhead** for up to a second, worse when slowed down: an older seek landed after a newer one and pulled the audio back. 774ms at 0.5x before, worst 22ms over 15 traced runs now.
- **Song select stuttered before the chart preview loaded**: 99ms median, 208ms worst on the GUI thread per switch, now 16ms and 27ms. Hashing, the background image and the `.osu` parse moved off the GUI thread.
- **Resuming after a long pause** in song select showed the playhead where the song would have been had it kept playing, until it snapped back.
- **Converting replaced whistle kats with new clap kats** at the playfield centre, losing their hitsound and their Fancy Arranger position (713 notes on one Ura Oni). A kat is whistle *or* clap.
- **A kat barline note right after an SV jump came apart**: its leading bars kept the speed from before the jump. Every line of a barline note now takes the note's speed.
