# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.4.3

App themes, and the first release delivered by the in-place updater.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2, click **Update and Restart**: the app downloads this version, checks it against the release's SHA-256, asks you to save unsaved difficulties, installs it into the folder you run from, and reopens. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## Added

- **Four app themes**, under **Settings → Skin → App theme**: **Pink** (the default, unchanged), **Taiko** (don-red buttons, kat-blue focus), **Matsuri** (warm lacquer black, vermilion and gold) and **Kiai** (navy with amber). A theme recolours the window around the chart only: the notes, the snap ticks, the SV graph's green and the star-rating colours stay osu!'s own. It applies after a restart, which the app offers on the spot.

## Changed

- **A faint white line marks every whole beat** down the full height of the chart and gimmick views. The snap ticks stay where they were: at the top and bottom, and through the middle on the fake slider layer.
- **The focused view has a rim** in the theme's colour, and its caption is in the accent. The rim had never actually been drawn.
- **The numbers on the SV, Kiai and Sound Volume and red line layers are bold**, and the SV and volume values are in the theme's accent.
- **The snap, time, timing bar, playback and mod controls sit on the app's own background** again, not the map's, on the Editor and Gimmick pages.

## Fixed

- **A one-word difficulty name too long for its header** ("worldwidesuperstar") ran under the view beside it. It wraps inside the header now, and the full name is the tooltip.
- **The timing bar flickered while a green line was dragged up or down.** Every step of the drag resized the bar to the chart's last note, and a timer stretched it back to the song's length a moment later, so every marker jumped sideways and back. The bar keeps both lengths now and draws at the longer one.
- **Switches, sliders and selection highlights kept Windows' accent colour** instead of the app's. They follow the theme now.
- **Spinners lit up yellow on every beat of a kiai section** in the gameplay preview. Only the notes and drumrolls pulse now, as in osu!.

- **The value labels on the SV and Kiai and Sound Volume layers were cut off at the top** when a point sat at the top of its range (100% volume, the highest SV on screen). A label that would run off the top is now drawn below its point.
