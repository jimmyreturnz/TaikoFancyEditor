# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.4.0

A redesign release. Song select, the editor shell, the Fancy Arranger and every settings dialog were rebuilt to approved mockups, and the gameplay preview now shows what osu! shows — its range, its draw order, and the mods.

## New

- **Mods on the Editor and Gimmick pages** — HD, NC / DT / DC / HT, HR / EZ and FL, in osu!'s own order. DT and HT stretch the song and keep its pitch; NC and DC resample it, so the pitch follows the rate, while the hitsounds stay at their own pitch. HR and EZ change scroll speed and drumroll length together (SliderMultiplier ×1.4×4/3 and ×0.8), HD fades each note out between a full scroll length and 0.625 of it, and FL is osu!'s radius and fall-off from `ppy/osu`. The chosen mods are remembered, and an outlined chip in the header shows them from every page.
- **Song select, redone** — a row per song with a dot per difficulty in osu!'s star colour, a banner of the selected difficulty's own background with a line that pulses on its own beat, star ratings read from osu!stable's `osu!.db` (24,183 maps in 270ms; a rating whose file has changed since is faded), and a Continue row of the charts you opened here. The song preview plays each difficulty's own audio from its PreviewTime, and keeps playing when you go back to the list from the editor, at 1.00× and normal pitch, from where the playhead was.
- **Select sounds** — the song list plays select-expand and select-difficulty, the skin's own when a skin has them.
- **Resnap** (Ctrl+R) — moves objects that sit a millisecond or two off their own snap back onto it, and a moved object now keeps its own snap: a 1/3 note dragged with 1/4 selected stays on 1/3.
- **Several difficulties on the Editor page** — one chart per difficulty from Add view, with page-wide zoom and view height, and a Hitsounds from choice for which one is heard.
- **Red line meter** on the barline layer's Red Line and Function tools, and **Omit barline at each note** for barline notes.

## Changed

- **Every settings dialog is a config sheet.** The barline layer's Config was one column of 13 numbers for three different tools with 28 pink buttons; it is now five sections with a list to jump between them. Every Config, Convert Notes and generator has a side panel that explains the field you are on and draws what the numbers write — the bars around a Don and a Kat, the anti-barline slits, the shiny stack beside a fake slider, the plot of every value Generate SV will write. Two values that clash turn both fields amber. Settings is dark like the rest of the app, with volume sliders and a shortcut search.
- **One look for every number, switch and scroll bar.** Numbers carry their unit and their step arrows inside the box instead of two pink squares beside it; checkboxes are switches; scroll bars are one thin handle, wider under the pointer and pink while held. SV boxes keep 8 decimals but drop trailing zeros — "1.00×" at rest, "1.4285714×" when that is what was typed.
- **The Fancy Arranger, as its mockup.** The canvas is in the middle at osu!'s proportions (the playfield is 80% of the height at osu!stable's (64, 56), and circles are osu!'s size at the export's CS); Transform and Timeline are docks you can move, float or close, and the layout is remembered. Shapes are picked from tiles with a line drawing each, parameters are a name and a number over a full-width slider, Split Don / Kat is a pair of pills beside Swap, and the notes glide to a new shape instead of jumping.
- **The editor shell.** Pink now marks the page, the tool, the speed, Play and Save, and nothing else — 49 filled pink controls on the Gimmick page before. Snap is a slider on every editor, each view's name and controls sit beside it, the view the tools act on has a pink rim, and the status bar holds the page's own settings.
- **The gameplay preview shows what osu! shows.** Its scale comes from its height and its range stops at a 16:9 screen's edge, as osu!'s does; a 1880-wide dock is 1880×275, where osu!'s lane at 1080p is 276. Objects are drawn in osu!'s order — the earlier one on top, ties in file order (`HitObjectContainer.Compare`), barlines underneath everything — so a shiny's note covers its fake sliders. A plain note flashes white in kiai and a shiny yellow, as in game.
- **Eased scrolling everywhere**, and the Exo 2 font drawn without ClearType colour fringes (709 → 104 fringe pixels on one song row).
- **Barline Don defaults to one bar after the note**, as most hand-made maps write it; Kat stays mirrored.
- **Downmixing a track is done in C**: 960ms → 70ms per track, and six charts open at once went from 34.0% to 2.7% of frames over budget right after loading.

## Fixed

- **The Fancy Arranger's toolbar ran off a 1920 screen** — it needed 1948px against 1904; now 1708px.
- **The fake slider generator's OK and Cancel sat in the middle of the dialog**, above its last three rows.
- **Number boxes cut off their own value** ("170." for 170.00): the box reserved room for its arrows twice.
- **A heavy gimmick section scrolled at under 60fps with no input at all** — Nbt-Hwt's hidden anti-barline wall: 100–109 of 240 frames late → 16–20 of 269, from a cheaper snap grid and one paint per wheel notch instead of two.
- **A closed window kept its render timer running**, which could hang the app's own tests and wasted a core per stale window.
- **An SV click placed an unrounded value** (1.2371…) while dragging rounded to 0.01; both round now.
- **Pasted SV points land on the snap**, and **a line dragged onto another of its own kind replaces it** instead of stacking two on one millisecond.
- **Text from a .osu file is shown as plain text**, so a difficulty named `<img src=…>` no longer renders as markup.

47 test modules, 1594 tests.
