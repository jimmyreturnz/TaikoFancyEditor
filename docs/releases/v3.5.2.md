# Taiko Fancy Arranger v3.5.2

An autoplay in the gameplay preview, so you can see the chart played the way a player hits it, plus drumroll hitsounds.

## Updating to this version

From v3.4.2 or later, click **Update and Restart**. From v3.4.1 or earlier, download it by hand once; see the v3.4.2 notes.

## New

- **Autoplay.** The editor's and the Gimmick page's gameplay previews now draw the skin's input drum (`taiko-bar-left`, `taiko-drum-inner`, `taiko-drum-outer`), and it is played as a player would play it. The **Autoplay** menu beside the mods picks the style (kddk full alternate, semi alternate, single tap, roll, or ddkk), the binding for kddk (1234, 1324, 4231) and the dominant hand. It starts on kddk full alternate. The hand rules come from a real player typing out how they hit test patterns, and those answers are part of the test suite. Song select's preview stays bare.
- **Experimental and feedback wanted.** Semi alternate is about 80% full alternate and 20% single tap, with runs of five or more notes starting on the dominant hand. It has no strict rule, so if a pattern comes out on the wrong hand for your style, send the pattern and how you would play it in 1234.
- **Kiai glow.** During kiai the skin's `taiko-glow` fades in behind the hit target and grows briefly on each hit, as in osu!.
- **A grey note sits in the hit target**: the skin's `taikobigcircle` at 22% opacity, as in osu!.
- **Drumrolls are heard.** A real drumroll plays a don on every tick, every 1/4 beat. These are the same ticks the autoplay hits. Fake sliders stay silent.

## Changed

- **The Experimental Conversion menu opens upward.**
- **The drum lights for 150ms per hit**, matching osu!'s `LegacyHalfDrum`.

## Fixed

- **The play button grew a little on every Play.** The text hidden under the drawn icon swapped to a wider "❚❚". It now keeps its width.

The full drum, glow and grey note cost nothing measurable: the preview paints in 2.01–2.04ms per frame with Autoplay on or off, at 1754px wide with a skin.
